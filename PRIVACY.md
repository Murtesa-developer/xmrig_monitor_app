# Privacy Policy

**XMRig Monitor** · Murtesa Developer

Last updated: 12 November 2026

## 1. Summary

The App has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The Developer does not collect, receive or sell your data.

## 2. What is stored on your device

To work, the App stores on your device: the nodes you add (name, IP address or hostname, port, access token, HTTPS setting), the last pool settings you entered (pool address, wallet address, pool password, rig ID), the path to your xmrig program, and whether you accepted these terms. This data is kept in the App’s local storage and is not encrypted by the App, so protect your device with a lock screen. Removing a node deletes it from the App; uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes everything.

## 3. Network connections

The App connects only to the nodes you add, using each miner’s HTTP API, to read status and, if you choose, to send configuration. The miner itself (not the App) connects to your pool. The App does not send any data to the Developer or to any other server. Connections to a node over plain HTTP are not encrypted, so use trusted networks or HTTPS.

## 4. Logs and files

Connection events and console output are kept in memory while the App is open. On desktop, when the App starts xmrig for you, it writes a config file (xmrig_config.json) to its own support folder; that file contains your pool, wallet and API token details.

## 5. Children

The App is not directed at children.

## 6. Third parties

The App does not include third-party analytics or advertising. XMRig and mining pools have their own privacy practices.

## 7. Your choices

You can delete any node in the Nodes tab, and delete all App data by clearing its storage or uninstalling it.

## 8. Responsibility

The Developer is not responsible for data exposed through a lost or compromised device, an unsafe network, or a miner that is misconfigured (see the Terms of Use).

## 9. Changes and contact

This policy may change with new versions of the App. Developer: Murtesa Developer.
